### 1. Echo & Relic Upgrade -- Quick Guide • There are 8 Echos, all

***Author:** s.63 \| Alinne | **Date:** 1/14/2026 2:25 PM*

Echo & Relic Upgrade -- Quick Guide • There are 8 Echos, all
unlocked through PvP • Echos levels are upgraded using Lightbreak
Ash • Echo quality is upgraded using Chaotic Crystals Lightbreak
Ash Sources: -- Playing PvP -- PvP Shop -- Weekly Packs Chaotic
Crystal Sources: -- Weekly Rank Reward Relic Upgrades: • Relic
upgrades are locked behind Echo levels • Your Echos must reach
certain levels before the Relic can be upgraded • The Relic
provides major stat boosts both in and out of PvP Important Relic
Thresholds: • Major boosts happen every 10 levels, starting at
Level 1 • 1 → 11 → 21 → 31 → etc • Level 31 unlocks a secondary
skill option • STR / AGI / INT are always active • PvP stats only
apply in PvP • Primary Stats (STR/AGI/INT) stay the same until you
hit the next 10-level threshold • PvP stats upgrade per Relic
level with the next upgrade being within the green parentheses.
Recommendation: • Everyone should aim for Relic Level 1 (very
easy) • Level 11 is easy to moderate depending on PvP results •
Level 21+ starts to get significantly expensive TL;DR: Upgrade all
Echos evenly, meet the required thresholds, then upgrade the Relic
at every 10-level impact point.

### 2. AFK This one is for levelling overnight, when you are

***Date:** 1/8/2026 6:27 AM*

AFK This one is for levelling overnight, when you are
working/studying, with the family/friends and all those moments
where you are not actively playing. You just place your Priest in
a good levelling place (refer to the guide for best places) and
hit AFK. The main focus with this build is just to reduce the
incoming damage, since later on food is a ENORMOUS gold sink, so I
prioritise receiving less damage and save some food more than
doing a little more damage. If gold is not an issue you can swap
some defensive skill for an offensive one (explained later) that
can grant a minor boost to XP/min For the defense we have PV:
Fortitude, Holy Fire, PV: Shield and Renew. The first three are
staples and reduce the incoming damage a lot, saving up food. The
later one, Renew, is the one I mentioned you can swap for Vampiric
Embrace or Shadowy Apparitions. Renew consumes a lot of mana, so
it's a damage loss because sometimes your toon will have to wait
couple of seconds for regenerate that mana and cast a damaging
spell. But is a lot of healing so it lets you go to a more
difficult camp earlier so that's more XP per kill. This is
personal preference and I would suggest to try one skill, AFK for
10 minutes, log back, check, swap it, AFK for another 10 minutes
and then compare your XP/min and food consumption and decide for
yourself. For attacking there are some staples too, like Holy
Shock and Shadowy Vortex, the main Area of Effect (AoE) we have.
Those are top priority so that's why they are that high. Then you
fulfil the skill bar with the next two damaging spells, SV: Gone
and Shadowflame. The last two skills are the personal choice as I
already said, with Renew, Vampiric Embrace and Shadowy
Apparitions. Or even Psychic Horror, is a 3 seconds "shield" every
30 seconds, so like a 10% mitigation. You can try all the
combinations for those last 2 slots between those 4 skills. It
will change every level and CP rating.


About Talents, it is very simple. Your focus should be on the
Shadowy Vortex and Holy Shock ones, those are the main priority.
Suffering makes Holy Shock apply to SV: Hurt, but until that point
since you are not casting Hurt at all, there is no reason to have
those Talents at lower levels. Up to level 30, respeccing is
completely free, so you can take advantage of it. At low levels
you don't even have all the skills, so just take Talents for the
ones you are using. Then, when a new skill unlocks, respect it. In
the end you will have enough Talents for fulfilling all the trees
just avoiding the less useful ones. For leveling you won't be
using Vampiric Touch at all so Harnessed Shadows and Improved Vamp
Touch have no use. Exactly as with Intangibility for Shadowmend,
is the one we are not using. For the first row, Haste is not that
useful until the Talents 41st to 45th are unlocked, so it is just
a filler after Talent 31st, don't take it early. So with 45
points, no paid Avatar and with just 1 from the Training Ground
everyone will eventually have the tree of the image. The leftovers
are placed into Deathwhisperer.


This spec is focused on a single target, like the Guild Boss event
or, mainly, the floors in Cloudland where you fight a single enemy
or even two bosses at the same time. Since there is no chaff, the
two staples from AFK, Holy Shock and Shadowy Vortex AoE aren't
useful. So we free the 6 Talent points from the Improved Vortex
and Suffering and place them into both Improved Vampirics, Touch
and Embrace hehehe About skills, those are long fights so Damage
over Time (DoT) are really strong. In a sustained fight they deal
tons of damage, so those boss fights are ideal for them to excel.
Once the Talents 41st to 45th are unlocked, the DoTs scale with
Haste, so it is better to apply the DoTs first and activate
Shadowform after it. With my current Haste, I manage to drop three
DoTS, Shadowform, Shadowflame, two Apparitions and then repeat the
Shadowflame and two Apparitions loop thrice. After that, reapply
the DoTs and rinse and repeat the Shadowflame plus two
Apparitions. Against the two bosses' floors, what I do is focus
on one first, apply all DoTs, then swap to the other and apply all
DoTs. After that I keep swapping the target to the one with more
health for casting Shadowflame and Apparitions against it. Renew
is last and since Apparitions has no Cool Down (CD) that means
it's gonna never be autocasted. Is there just for manual cast if
the incoming damage is very high.


ADDS This is a hybrid of both above. Some floors have a big boss
but they come with some chaff, so I want both the DoTs for the
boss but also the AoEs for the chaff. So I ended up with this
mixed build, where Holy Shock applies Hurt thanks to Suffering. I
start fights like this with all the skills on autocast, but when
chaff is about 10~15% of health remaining, I turn off both
Shadowy Vortex and Holy Shock, and I just cast Holy Shock manually
whenever Hurt wears off from the boss. That alone also kills the
ads while maximising the damage against the boss. These flops are
usually very hard, because if you focus too much on AoEs for the
chaff, you are short on single damage against the boss and the
time runs out. But the chaff does lots of damage, so not killing
then means more Renews (again, last for manually cast) and that's
a DPS lost too. About Talents, the Shadowflame chain is the one we
must skip for taking both Vortex/Suffering for adds and Improved
Touch and Embrace. This one is still being tested, so I'm up for
constructive criticism.

### 3. RUNES Whenever you reach high levels everything starts to get

***Date:** 1/8/2026 9:41 AM*

RUNES Whenever you reach high levels everything starts to get
expensive: upgrading or upstarring the shirt takes a lot of silk,
enhancing a lot of pieces, fetching for good refinements a lot of
stones... but there is one thing that can improve a lot the
performance without that high investment: the Runes. We can get
three free attempts each day, and then we can use Scrolls for
more. Those Scrolls are rewarded at Cloudland whenever you clear a
X0 or X5 level (5, 10, 15, 20...) so that's why progressing into
Cloudland is that important and why I have dedicated specs for
single boss, dual boss and boss with adds. Currently I'm 6th in my
server in Solo Leaderboard, with all the other characters above me
with many more levels and CP than me, so it is very satisfying
being that high as a low spender myself. And I'm ranking 4th at
Three Player Leaderboard, again against players with more money
dropped into their accounts. So with good planning, skill rotation
and awareness, Cloudland can be done. But there are some gear
checks, where even with everything perfectly done, the boss has
too much health so with your current gear and level it is just
impossible to beat. Don't get frustrated if that happens. About
getting the Runes, the best is to grind Cloudland as said and save
up all the Scrolls for when Priest weekend arises. Every week, on
Thursday, Friday and Saturday an "event" called Class Pool
arrives, and you can choose one class between three that are
offered. If you select Priest, then you can dump all your Scrolls
and you are guaranteed to get Priest Runes like in the screenshots
below.

### 5. Gear Replacement Let's make one thing very clear. Higher GS and

***Date:** 1/13/2026 12:00 AM*

Gear Replacement Let's make one thing very clear. Higher GS and
little green arrows does not always mean you should upgrade gear.
It can sometimes be misleading and actually lead to a loss of
power and dps. One of the biggest things that the game does not
take into account with individual gear upgrades is for the
potential loss of set bonuses that may occur due to swapping out a
piece. So you have to use caution when tossing new items into
the mix. A lot of times the game will also consider a new item an
upgrade simply because one stat, even if it is irrelevant for you,
is higher than a current stat. For example, you can have a piece
with good versatility and crit on it, but if a new piece has
higher dodge on it than either of your existing stats, the game is
likely to recommend it as an upgrade. You equip it and will
actually see that you lose CP. So just pay attention when you are
clicking through all those little green arrows to not replace a
solid piece of gear based on the game's recommendation. With that
in mind, let's look at our stats so you feel better prepared and
can make the right decision.
STAT PRIO As always, we are stuck behind the traditional hit and
expertise caps, both of which will continue to increase for new
content, but should remain the same for older content. So for raid
and Mythic + dungeons you want to have a minimum of 20% hit and
15% expertise. If you have more than that it's perfectly fine as
we have already seen an increase in these requirements with the MC
Abyss raids up to "expertise is 20% for abyss, 25% for mc3 last
boss btw." -s2.CodeGreen. One of the best ways to do this is to
push as many of your collections items for hit and expertise that
still pertain to your "Main" class. Ie Agility for hunters. Since
all classes will need to meet these thresholds it only makes sense
to do that here and supplement the rest of the hit and expertise
from your gear. Once we have met the minimum thresholds for hit
and expertise, our stat priority is: Versatility > Crit > Hit >
Expertise While you can reforge a piece of gear to hope for bis
stats, it is quite expensive so sometimes it is easier to wait for
a different piece to drop and use the heritage to move your
affixes over to it. If a piece has versatility and any of the
other 3 attributes I'm pretty happy with it. Top plays will be
versatile and critical.
Why versatility over crit chance? Versatility is just that good.
For every percentage that you increase versatility, you increase
every point of damage by that same percentage. You even get the
added bonus of .5% damage reduction on top of it. Survivability
matters! Where crit on the other hand is a percent chance that you
will do a flat 150% damage. On a point by point basis, you would
need a 100% crit chance to match 50% versatility. That's not to
say that crit is useless as most classes, esp hunter, have some
core talents that synergize very heavily with our ability to crit.
(We'll dive into those in a bit)
Tier Stats (Refinable) We have a LOT of options depending on the
gear slot. The percent increases are ALWAYS the first thing I hunt
for (no pun intended) when refining. Here is a breakdown of how I
prioritize these: Helm; Shoulders; Chest; Legs;
% AP > AGI > AP > STA Necklace; % Phys DMG Bonus > % Skill DMG
> AGI > AP Cloak; % Skill DMG > AGI > AP > STA Bracers;
Gloves; Belt; Boots; % HP > AGI > AP > STA Rings; Weapon % Phys
DMG Bonus > % Skill DMG > % Crit DMG > AGI Trinkets: % Skill
DMG > % Crit DMG > % Armor Pen > % Mana Regen Prioritizing
stats in a manner similar to this will yield great results.
The one I am most hesitant on is the Trinket with the Armor Pen
being larger than the Mana Regen. Cunning Strike is our filler
ability. I have personally found with the uptime of other
abilities, it is very rare that I cast Cunning Strike so much that
I am mana starved when my next ability comes off of CD. I do have
the rune that reduces the cost of Cunning Strike, but I do not
currently have % Mana regen on either of my trinkets. If having
Cunning Strike on your bar is reducing your DPS because you are
mana starved, you may need to refine for % Mana Regen or be forced
to purchase the epic version of the rune.
Skills and Runes There are several really solid builds floating
around out there, but in all my testing I have yet to replicate a
build that performs as well in high level M+. Testing damage on a
dummy is one thing, but dragging mobs through mist while avoiding
boss aoe so you don't get one shot changes things up a lot,
especially our rune selection. For runes we use: (Skill Name :
Rune Name) Nature's Protection : Porcupine The alternative
provides little damage increase for survivability. We get more
utility from Porcupine Scatter Shot : Dynamite Dynamite not only
gives mana reduction, but also increases our vs single targets
significantly. Nature's Companion : Lone Wolf The alternative
Companionship is really defense oriented. Lone Wolf wins here.
Survival Instincts : Self-Mend For Mythic+ either option is rather
viable, but Self-Mend provides that shield for 20% of your HP for
when you've got a mob on you. Blast Trap : Fissure I love the idea
of Concussion. Reducing the cooldown of one of our biggest AOE
abilities is YUGE; however, in Mythic+ you CAN NOT risk a poorly
timed stun. The damage of Fissure is still really strong though
and does make us a little stronger on a single target. Cunning
Strike : Ambush The reduced mana and chance to reset Blast Trap
make this an instant choice. If you have mana regen % on your
trinkets you could play around with Ambush and Vigilance to see
what yields better results for you. Skylord's Strike : Vanish For
M+ the damage output of Rip falls off too much when we are
fighting bosses. The consistent damage throughput of Vanish makes
it come out on top. Wing Clip : Feathered Arrow This is one I have
had the hardest time testing. What I have found in high level M+
is that pulling smaller packs so your tank doesn't die is quite
vital. So in combination with that and boss fights being single
target, we get a nice damage boost to Wing Clip through Feather
Arrow
Falcon's Fury : Tranquility Falcon's Fury already hits 5 targets,
so taking a dmg loss to cleave more mobs with Audacity isn't
justified in the higher tiers of M+/ Furthermore, Tranquility
increases damage to bosses which is a big plus. Hawk's Blessing :
Eagle's Cry One of the biggest benefits of Eagle's Cry is that it
also triggers a Wing Clip to go off. One day I'll have the
legendary for both Hawk's Blessing runes, and can do more thorough
testing.
Talent Choices Now that we've laid the groundwork we can walk
through the skill tree. While playing around with builds there are
several talents that are staples. Ones that you shouldn't go
without. As a reminder, this is a build for high-floor Mythic +
there are some talents that you would choose for other varieties
of the game like Abyss Runs or PVP. This is also a good place to
note that there are additional talent points that can be acquired
through the Training Center, so I am currently at 57 talent points
from that and skins.
Tier 1: Improved Cunning Strike: 3/3 More DMG Survival Instincts:
0/3 Don't waste 3 Talent points on 1.5% DMG Reduction. (3%
Versatility does that) Improved Guard: 2/2 20% AtK is BIG! Tier 2:
Venomous Spear: 3/3 Crits = Poison; Poison increases Crit 3% -
Great for bosses when kitting Improved Scatter Shot: 3/3 More DMG
Improved Skylord's Strike More DMG Tier 3: Spear of Vengeance: 0/3
These talents top us out at 18% chance to reset our Skylord's CD.
This is pretty nutty, but we want to save these talent points for
something even better down the road. This is one of the skills
that I have the hardest time passing up. Venomous Scatter: 3/3
While we already have a method to apply poison with Cunning
Strike, but that is only when it crits. We want to ensure we get
our poison up. Good poison uptime can be more than 10% of our
